Primary and secondary active transport

As you watch, identify what supplies the energy at each protein and which substance is moved against its gradient.

Sodium–potassium pump

Primary active transport
Immediate energy source
ATP hydrolysis by the pump
Moved uphill
3 sodium ions out and 2 potassium ions in per ATP

Sodium–glucose cotransporter

Secondary active transport
Immediate energy source
2 sodium ions entering down their electrochemical gradient
Moved uphill
1 glucose molecule into the cell against its concentration gradient

The cotransporter shown uses two sodium ions for each glucose molecule, as in SGLT1.

How the two proteins work together

ATP powers the pump. The pump maintains the sodium gradient. Sodium entry through the cotransporter then drives glucose uptake.
