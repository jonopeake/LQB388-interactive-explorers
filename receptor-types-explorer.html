Both acetylcholine examples change ion movement. Which receptor is a ligand-gated ion channel, and how can you tell?

Show explanation

The nicotinic receptor is a ligand-gated ion channel. Acetylcholine binds to the protein that forms the pore. The muscarinic receptor activates a G protein that changes the activity of a separate ion channel.
