Prediction challenge

Set the concentration to 20 mM with three carriers. Which change will increase the initial transport rate more: doubling glucose or doubling carriers? Use the sliders to compare 40 mM with three carriers and 20 mM with six carriers.

Show answer

Doubling carriers has the larger effect. At 20 mM the existing carriers are already cycling near capacity. Adding carriers increases the number that can transport glucose at the same time and raises the maximum rate.
