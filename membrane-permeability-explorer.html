Keep lipid solubility high and change the molecule from small to large. Then switch to ion channels and close them all. Why does net transport change in each case?

Show answer

A larger molecule generally diffuses less readily through the bilayer when lipid solubility is held constant. Closing the channels removes the pathway for the ion in this model. Both comparisons start with the same concentration difference. The more permeable setting lets more particles enter over the same interval.
