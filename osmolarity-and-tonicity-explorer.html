Show explanation

Osmolarity counts all dissolved particles. Tonicity depends on solutes that sustain an osmotic difference across the membrane. Urea enters the cell and water follows. The non-permeating solutes stay on their own sides. When present at equal concentrations, they produce no sustained change in cell volume.
