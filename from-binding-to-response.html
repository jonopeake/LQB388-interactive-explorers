Optional comparison: receptor affinity

Assume two cells have the same number of receptors, the same free agonist concentration and no competitor. Which would generally have a larger fraction of its receptors occupied at equilibrium?

Show explanation

The cell with higher-affinity receptors would generally have a larger fraction occupied. Affinity describes binding. The resulting response also depends on how effectively binding activates the receptor and how the cell relays that activation.
