Follow the signal

Choose a route. Follow the signal from its source to its target and see what crosses the space between cells.

Blue electrical signal along a neuron · Amber chemical messenger · Purple ions through a gap junction · Green outline responding cell

Compare the distances. In synaptic signalling, the electrical signal travels along the axon and the neurotransmitter crosses only the narrow cleft. In endocrine signalling, the hormone itself travels through blood to distant cells.
